ASSET CREDITS
=============

assets/farm/  and  assets/ui/
-----------------------------
Sliced from Asset1.png and Asset2.png, the two sprite sheets supplied
by the project owner in Images/. Both sheets already carried a clean
alpha channel; this project only cut them into named pieces and
trimmed each to its bounding box. No recolouring or redrawing.

  farm/  4 plot tiles (empty / locked / ready / grown),
         5 crops x 4 growth stages (pumpkin, corn, tomato, carrot,
         wheat), and props (fence, lantern fence, signpost, banner,
         rock, haystack)
  ui/    HUD chrome (profile bar, coin pill, shop, chest, book,
         mission ribbon, diamond buttons, farmer portrait) and the
         six mutated-crop sprites

  Provenance of those two sheets is the owner's to confirm before any
  public release. They were provided as project input.

assets/crop/
------------
Nineteen produce icons (pepper, eggplant, grape, cherries, banana,
pineapple, coconut, avocado, lemon, orange, pear, broccoli, cauliflower,
cabbage, mushroom, leek, garlic, radish, beetroot) are drawn procedurally
for this project by Tools/gen_produce.py, 256 px.

The remaining files (bread, cheese, egg, honey, lime, salad, potato, corn,
peach, strawberry, watermelon) are still Kenney "Food Kit" previews —
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain), https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
— and are either unused or covered by painted art at runtime.

Resources/Art/items/
--------------------
Shop item icons and the farm coin, drawn procedurally for this project by
Tools/gen_items.py.

Reference screenshots
---------------------
Images/Screenshot_*.jpg are screenshots of Garena's "Lien Quan Mobile"
farm mini-game. They were used only to match LAYOUT and interaction
design. No pixel from them ships in this project.

Resources/Fonts/  (Baloo 2)
---------------------------
Baloo 2 by The Baloo 2 Project Authors (Ek Type) — SIL Open Font License 1.1
(full text: Resources/Fonts/Baloo2-OFL.txt).
  https://github.com/EkType/Baloo2
Static SemiBold (600), Bold (700) and ExtraBold (800) instances cut from the
variable font by Tools/make_fonts.py (fontTools): subset to Latin and
Vietnamese, unitsPerEm and vertical metrics changed so it sits at Nunito's
size. The licence declares no Reserved Font Name; the modified family is
renamed "Baloo 2 MiT" inside the files anyway, so it is never mistaken for
the original. The OFL allows bundling in a game; the font may not be sold
on its own.

Resources/Audio/  (sound effects)
---------------------------------
Synthesised from scratch for this project by Tools/gen_sfx.py
(2026-09-15): water, coins, error, claim, mutation, chest_open,
plank_0..2, level_up, island_unlock, legendary. Sine partials (marimba,
kalimba, bell, harp), filtered noise (leaves, soil, air) and a small
algorithmic room — no samples. They replace the Kenney CC0 audio used
until then (same file names), so no Kenney sound ships any more.
Resources/Audio/Kenney-License.txt stays: the Kenney CC0 images elsewhere
(Art/ui2, Art/crop) are under the same licence.
(cine_whoosh.ogg and cine_chime.ogg: see below.)

Resources/Audio/tap, tab, toggle, menu_open, menu_close, panel_open,
panel_close, harvest_0/1, plant_0/1 .ogg  (the sounds that answer a touch)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Built by Tools/make_press_sfx.py (2026-09-15, second round) from recorded
mouth pops:
  "Pop sounds" by cogitollc — OpenGameArt.org, posted 2022-07-14
  https://opengameart.org/content/pop-sounds
  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
  Files used: pop4.ogg, pop6.ogg, pop7.ogg, pop9.ogg, pop10.ogg (sha1s in
  the script). Author's note: "Pop sounds created for our first game, you
  can use it too" — recorded on an iPhone SE (Voice Memos, Ocenaudio).
Processed, not used as is: trimmed, resampled to pitch (A-flat major
pentatonic), layered, low-passed, levelled. The panel sounds add
synthesised air (gen_sfx.swish); harvest and plant add gen_sfx's
synthesised soil and leaf noise. The originals are not in the repo;
--fetch downloads them. Credit is not required by CC0; given here anyway.

Resources/Audio/Music/terrace_in_the_clouds.ogg  (background music)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
From Terrace_in_the_Clouds.mp3, supplied by the project owner (repo root).
Tools/make_music.py only trims the ending into a seamless loop and lowers the
level; no notes changed. The mp3 carries no title/artist/copyright tags,
only a C2PA manifest signed by Google stating "Created by Google Generative
AI" (IPTC digitalSourceType trainedAlgorithmicMedia) with a SynthID
watermark, time-stamped 2026-09-15. So it is AI-generated with a Google
tool. Rights to use it in a released game depend on that tool's terms and
the owner's account — the owner must confirm before any public release.
Fine for test builds.

Resources/Art/bridge/, Resources/Art/sky/
-----------------------------------------
Drawn procedurally for this project (Tools/gen_bridge.py, Tools/gen_sky.py).
The clouds (cumulus_*, sea_*, cirrus, cloud_noise) are numpy paintings of
soft ellipsoid lobes and noise, lit at runtime by Resources/Shaders/UICloud

the owner-supplied Art/farm/fence_lamp.png — same provenance caveat).

Resources/Art/life/, Resources/Art/snow/, Resources/Art/beds/bed_frost.png,

Resources/Art/decor/, Assets/Scripts/Farm/DecorCatalog.cs
---------------------------------------------------------
Painted yard props (well, windmill, market stall, clay oven, campfire, crystal
cluster, giant mushroom, lanterns, signs, barrels, carts, …) cut by
Tools/slice_decor.py from four sheets the project owner generated with
ChatGPT (OpenAI) on 2026-09-15 and placed at the repo root:
"ChatGPT Image Sep 15, 2026, 06_49_03 PM.png", "… 06_49_15 PM.png",
"… 06_49_24 PM.png", "… 06_49_33 PM.png". This project only cut, cleaned the
alpha and edge colours, padded them to a ground pivot, added contact shadows
and recoloured the grass skirts to each island's ground (snow, ash, slate,
sand) and one crystal to ice; the windmill's sails were lifted off
(mill_sails.png, four copies of one painted sail) and the roof and tower behind
them rebuilt from the visible paint; the light masks (*_glow), the snow caps
and the skirt snow overlays in Art/snow/ are computed from those cuts (the
skirt overlays of Art/farm haystack, rock, signpost and banner likewise). AI-generated art provided by the
owner as project input: usage rights for a public release are the owner's to
confirm, like the other owner-provided sheets above. No third-party decor
pack was downloaded: the free CC0 sets considered (Kenney's farm/nature kits,
OpenGameArt isometric farm tiles) are flat vector or pixel styles that do not
sit next to these paintings.
