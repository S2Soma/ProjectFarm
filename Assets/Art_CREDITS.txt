ASSET CREDITS
=============

assets/farm/  and  assets/ui/
-----------------------------
Sliced from Asset1.png and Asset2.png, the two sprite sheets supplied
by the project owner in Images/. Both sheets already carried a clean
alpha channel; this project only cut them into named pieces and
trimmed each to its bounding box. No recolouring or redrawing.

  farm/  4 plot tiles (empty / locked / ready / grown),
         5 crops x 4 growth stages (pumpkin, corn, tomato, carrot,
         wheat), and props (fence, lantern fence, signpost, banner,
         rock, haystack)
  ui/    HUD chrome (profile bar, coin pill, shop, chest, book,
         mission ribbon, diamond buttons, farmer portrait) and the
         six mutated-crop sprites

  Provenance of those two sheets is the owner's to confirm before any
  public release. They were provided as project input.

assets/crop/
------------
Kenney "Food Kit" — Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
Used for the produce icons of the crops the painted sheet does not
cover. Previews/*.png, trimmed and upscaled 2x.

Reference screenshots
---------------------
Images/Screenshot_*.jpg are screenshots of Garena's "Lien Quan Mobile"
farm mini-game. They were used only to match LAYOUT and interaction
design. No pixel from them ships in this project.
